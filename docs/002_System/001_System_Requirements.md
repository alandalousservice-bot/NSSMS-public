# NSSMS — System Requirements

## Functional Requirements

### FR-001 — Seasons
The system shall support the lifecycle management of sports seasons.

### FR-002 — Competitions
The system shall support creation, management, monitoring, and closure of competitions.

### FR-003 — Digital Licenses
The system shall support digital sports licenses.

### FR-004 — QR Verification
The system shall provide a mechanism for verifying a digital license using QR.

### FR-005 — Audit
The system shall record defined auditable operations.

### FR-006 — Non-Destructive Lifecycle
The system shall prevent permanent deletion of governed official records.

### FR-007 — Public Portal
The system shall expose approved public information.

### FR-008 — Management Dashboards
The system shall provide administrative dashboards.

### FR-009 — Access Control
The system shall restrict administrative operations based on authorization.

### FR-010 — Reporting
The system shall support reporting requirements defined during analysis.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Security.
- Integrity.
- Availability.
- Maintainability.
- Performance.
- Scalability.
- Auditability.
- Accessibility.
- Localization.
- Backup and recovery.
