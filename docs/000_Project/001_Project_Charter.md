# NSSMS — Project Charter

**Version:** 0.2  
**Status:** Working Baseline  
**Source:** Master Project Specification WIP

## 1. Project Purpose
NSSMS is intended to become the official national digital platform for managing school sports in Algeria.

## 2. Objectives
- Digitize school-sports administration.
- Manage sports seasons and competitions.
- Provide digital licenses with QR verification.
- Preserve historical records.
- Maintain an audit trail.
- Provide public information and administrative dashboards.
- Establish a foundation for future integrations and services.

## 3. Scope
### Included
- Project and business analysis.
- System requirements.
- UI/UX.
- Database design.
- API planning.
- Security.
- Testing.
- Deployment planning.
- Seasons.
- Competitions.
- Digital licenses.
- QR verification.
- Audit.
- Public portal.
- Management dashboards.

### Not Yet Defined
- Final technology stack.
- Exact administrative hierarchy.
- Complete role matrix.
- Detailed competition rules.
- External integrations.
- Hosting model.
- Detailed retention periods.

## 4. Principles
- Regulatory compliance.
- Governmental identity.
- Data integrity.
- Traceability.
- Non-destructive record lifecycle.
- Security by design.
- Separation of public and administrative access.
- Extensibility.

## 5. Initial Stakeholders
- National administration.
- Regional/Wilaya administration.
- Educational institutions.
- Schools.
- Students/athletes.
- Competition officials.
- System administrators.
- Public users.

## 6. Success Criteria
The project should provide a validated business model, traceable requirements, secure administration, digital licensing, auditability, historical preservation, public information, and implementation-ready technical documentation.

## 7. Open Decisions
All unspecified governance, permissions, workflows, integrations, technical choices, and retention rules remain subject to analysis and approval.
