# NSSMS — Project Vision

## Vision Statement
إنشاء النظام الوطني لتسيير الرياضة المدرسية في الجزائر كمنصة حكومية رسمية.

## Strategic Vision
NSSMS should provide one coherent national digital environment for school-sports governance and operations.

## Strategic Pillars
### 1. Official Regulation
System workflows must reflect approved regulations.

### 2. National Governance
The platform should support centralized visibility while allowing defined administrative levels to operate within their authority.

### 3. Competition Management
Seasons and competitions should have structured lifecycles and traceable records.

### 4. Digital Identity
Sports licenses should be represented digitally and support QR-based verification.

### 5. Accountability
Important operations must be auditable.

### 6. Historical Preservation
Official records must not be permanently deleted when preservation is required.

### 7. Public Transparency
Approved information should be available through a public portal.

## Long-Term Outcome
The intended outcome is a trusted national platform that reduces fragmented processes and creates reliable, traceable school-sports information.
