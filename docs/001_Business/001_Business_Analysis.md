# NSSMS — Business Analysis

## 1. Business Context
NSSMS is a proposed official national platform for school sports in Algeria.

## 2. Business Objectives
- Apply official regulations.
- Centralize seasons and competitions.
- Digitize sports licensing.
- Enable QR verification.
- Maintain auditability.
- Preserve historical information.
- Provide public and administrative access.

## 3. Stakeholder Categories
| Category | Expected Responsibility |
|---|---|
| National administration | Governance and national oversight |
| Regional/Wilaya administration | Regional administration |
| Educational institutions | Institutional participation |
| Schools | School-level operations |
| Students/Athletes | Participation |
| Officials | Competition operations |
| Administrators | System operations |
| Public | Access to approved public data |

## 4. Business Capabilities
- Manage seasons.
- Manage competitions.
- Manage organizations.
- Manage participants.
- Issue/manage licenses.
- Verify licenses.
- Publish approved information.
- Monitor operations.
- Audit actions.
- Produce reports.

## 5. Business Rules — Baseline
- Official regulations apply.
- Digital licenses use QR.
- Important actions are auditable.
- Permanent deletion is prohibited for governed records.
