# NSSMS — Organizational Model

## 1. Purpose
This document defines a **logical organizational model** for NSSMS. It is not a legal statement of the Algerian administrative hierarchy. The exact authority structure must be validated against official organizational documents.

## 2. Logical Levels

```text
National Governance
        │
        ▼
Regional / Wilaya Administration
        │
        ▼
Educational Institutions / Schools
        │
        ▼
Participants / Teams / Activities
```

## 3. Responsibilities

### National Level
Potential responsibilities:
- National configuration.
- National season governance.
- National competition oversight.
- Policy and regulatory configuration.
- National reporting.

### Regional / Wilaya Level
Potential responsibilities:
- Regional competition administration.
- Local validation.
- Monitoring schools and activities.
- Regional reporting.

### Institution / School Level
Potential responsibilities:
- Participant registration.
- Team/activity management.
- Submission of required information.
- Monitoring participation.

### Competition Officials
Potential responsibilities:
- Competition operations.
- Results entry.
- Validation of competition records.

## 4. Authority Principle
A user must only be able to perform an operation that belongs to the organizational level and role assigned to that user.

## 5. Data Visibility Principle
Visibility should follow the user's scope of authority unless a record is explicitly designated as public.
