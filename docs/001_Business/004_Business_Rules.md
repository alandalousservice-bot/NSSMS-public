# NSSMS — Business Rules

## Confirmed From Master Specification
**BR-001:** Official regulations must be applied.

**BR-002:** The platform shall use a governmental Algerian visual identity.

**BR-003:** Seasons and competitions must be manageable.

**BR-004:** Digital licenses shall support QR.

**BR-005:** An audit trail shall exist.

**BR-006:** Permanent deletion shall not be used for governed records.

**BR-007:** The platform shall provide a public portal and management dashboards.

## Proposed Rules Requiring Approval
**BR-P001:** Major workflows should have defined approval states.

**BR-P002:** Public information should require an approved publication state.

**BR-P003:** Historical records should be archived rather than destroyed.

These proposed rules must be validated before implementation.
