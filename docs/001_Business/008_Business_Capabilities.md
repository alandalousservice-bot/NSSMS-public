# NSSMS — Business Capability Map

## Level 1 Capabilities

| ID | Capability | Description | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| BC-01 | Governance | National administration and configuration | High |
| BC-02 | Identity & Access | Users, roles and permissions | High |
| BC-03 | Organization Management | Institutions and administrative scope | High |
| BC-04 | Season Management | Sports seasons and lifecycle | High |
| BC-05 | Competition Management | Competitions and participation | High |
| BC-06 | Participant Management | Participants and eligibility data | High |
| BC-07 | Digital Licensing | Digital licenses and lifecycle | High |
| BC-08 | QR Verification | Public/authorized license verification | High |
| BC-09 | Results | Competition results | Medium |
| BC-10 | Reporting | Operational and management reporting | Medium |
| BC-11 | Audit | Traceability of important operations | High |
| BC-12 | Public Information | Approved public content | Medium |

## Capability Maturity
Each capability shall progress through:
1. Business definition.
2. Requirements.
3. Data definition.
4. UI definition.
5. API definition.
6. Testing.
7. Implementation.

## Rule
No capability should be implemented solely from a high-level name. Its business owner, workflows, rules, permissions, data, and acceptance criteria must be defined.
