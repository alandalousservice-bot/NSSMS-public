# NSSMS — Detailed Business Requirements

## BR-REQ-001 — Regulatory Compliance
NSSMS shall represent approved school-sports rules in its business processes.

**Rationale:** The master specification explicitly identifies application of official regulations as a project pillar.

**Acceptance:** Each implemented regulated workflow has a documented source rule or approved business decision.

## BR-REQ-002 — Season Governance
NSSMS shall support controlled creation, management, activation, closure, and historical preservation of sports seasons.

## BR-REQ-003 — Competition Governance
NSSMS shall support controlled competition creation, review, operation, results, closure, and historical preservation.

## BR-REQ-004 — Digital License
NSSMS shall support a digital representation of a sports license.

## BR-REQ-005 — QR Verification
NSSMS shall support QR-based verification of a license.

## BR-REQ-006 — Auditability
NSSMS shall maintain an audit trail for defined sensitive and administrative operations.

## BR-REQ-007 — Non-Destructive Records
Governed official records shall not be permanently deleted.

## BR-REQ-008 — Public Access
NSSMS shall provide approved public information through a public portal.

## BR-REQ-009 — Administrative Dashboards
NSSMS shall provide dashboards for authorized management users.

## BR-REQ-010 — Controlled Authority
Administrative actions shall be restricted according to organizational scope and assigned permissions.

## BR-REQ-011 — Historical Integrity
Closed or archived records shall remain retrievable according to authorized access rules.

## BR-REQ-012 — Traceable Publication
Public information shall only be exposed after the required approval/publishing process is completed.

## Status
BR-REQ-001 through BR-REQ-009 are directly grounded in the master specification at a high level. BR-REQ-010 through BR-REQ-012 are detailed system interpretations that require formal validation.
