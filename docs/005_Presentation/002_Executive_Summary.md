# NSSMS — Executive Summary

NSSMS is a proposed national governmental platform for school sports in Algeria.

The project focuses on applying official regulations through a structured digital platform that manages seasons and competitions, supports digital QR-based sports licenses, preserves an audit trail, avoids permanent deletion of governed records, and provides both a public portal and administrative dashboards.

The current phase establishes the project documentation baseline. Detailed requirements, governance, workflows, security, database schema, UI, API, testing, and deployment decisions remain subject to formal analysis and approval.
